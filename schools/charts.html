The money

Ohio law holds voted levies to the dollars they raised when they passed, so districts well above the 20-mill floor, as CH-UH is, must return to voters as costs rise. The forecast shows when the money runs short.

Spending & staff

CH-UH spends well above the state average on every measure. The staffing record supports both sides: the district has cut teachers faster than enrollment fell, while support staff grew.

Who the district serves

Enrollment has fallen by a third since 2000. More of the district's resident students now use state scholarships to attend private schools, and about a third of resident children are in private school or homeschool.

Results

Scores are below the state but have risen quickly since 2023-24. Adjusted for child poverty, CH-UH performs about as expected. Different growth measures disagree.
