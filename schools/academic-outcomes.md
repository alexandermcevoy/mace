# Academic Outcomes: The Report Card Record

*Cleveland Heights-University Heights City School District (CH-UH). Research current to October 12, 2026. Issue 4, a 6.5-mill continuing operating levy, is on the November 3, 2026 ballot. This page covers the district's academic record only. Finances, demographics and a demographically-adjusted analysis are on separate pages.*

> **About the sources.** We could not open the Ohio Department of Education and Workforce (DEW) report card site directly while writing this page. Most of the component ratings below come from **the district's own report card announcements** on chuh.org. Where a news outlet (cleveland.com, News 5 Cleveland) reported the same figure separately, we say so. Figures we could only infer, or could not verify, are labeled as such and listed under [Gaps / unverified](#gaps--unverified). Readers can check every number against the state's official site, [reportcard.education.ohio.gov](https://reportcard.education.ohio.gov) [1].

## Key facts

- **Overall rating, 2025-26: 3.5 of 5 stars**, the same as 2024-25 ([CH-UH, Sept. 15, 2026](https://www.chuh.org/p/~board/district-board-posts/post/ch-uh-announces-report-card-gains-for-2025-2026) [2]; also reported by [cleveland.com via NewsBreak, 2026](https://www.newsbreak.com/cleveland-com-512965/4889945722889-cleveland-heights-university-heights-maintains-3-5-stars-ohio-school-report-cards-2026) [3]). In Ohio's system, 3 stars or more means "meets state standards."
- **Overall rating, 2024-25: 3.5 stars, up from 2.5 stars in 2023-24** ([CH-UH, 2025](https://www.chuh.org/p/~board/district-board-posts/post/ch-uh-announces-report-card-gains-for-2024-2025) [4]; also reported by [News 5 Cleveland, 2025](https://www.news5cleveland.com/news/local-news/ohio-school-report-cards-released-east-cleveland-and-akron-public-school-districts-see-improvements) [5]). In 2023-24 the district was **below** the "meets standards" line ([News 5, 2024](https://www.news5cleveland.com/news/local-news/see-how-well-your-school-scored-ohio-releases-2023-2024-report-cards) [6]).
- **Performance Index (PI) rose three years in a row: 69.2 (2023-24), 74.6 (2024-25), 79 (2025-26)** ([CH-UH 2025](https://www.chuh.org/p/~board/district-board-posts/post/ch-uh-announces-report-card-gains-for-2024-2025) [4]; [CH-UH 2026](https://www.chuh.org/p/~board/district-board-posts/post/ch-uh-announces-report-card-gains-for-2025-2026) [2]). The district calls 79 its highest PI in six years.
- **Achievement went from 2 stars (2023-24 and 2024-25) to 3 stars (2025-26).** Achievement measures how many students pass state tests, and it is the district's weakest component over this period ([CH-UH 2025](https://www.chuh.org/p/~board/district-board-posts/post/ch-uh-announces-report-card-gains-for-2024-2025) [4]; [CH-UH 2026](https://www.chuh.org/p/~board/district-board-posts/post/ch-uh-announces-report-card-gains-for-2025-2026) [2]).
- **Progress (value-added growth) went to 4 stars and Gap Closing to 5 stars in 2025-26**, each up one star from the year before ([CH-UH 2026](https://www.chuh.org/p/~board/district-board-posts/post/ch-uh-announces-report-card-gains-for-2025-2026) [2]).
- **Third-grade reading:** 64% of third graders read at or above grade level in 2024-25 and **68.9% in 2025-26**. The Early Literacy component stayed at 3 stars both years ([CH-UH 2026](https://www.chuh.org/p/~board/district-board-posts/post/ch-uh-announces-report-card-gains-for-2025-2026) [2]).
- **Weighted graduation rate:** 92.4% in 2024-25 and **93.0% in 2025-26**. The Graduation component stayed at 3 stars ([CH-UH 2025](https://www.chuh.org/p/~board/district-board-posts/post/ch-uh-announces-report-card-gains-for-2024-2025) [4]; [CH-UH 2026](https://www.chuh.org/p/~board/district-board-posts/post/ch-uh-announces-report-card-gains-for-2025-2026) [2]).
- **Not verified for this page:** proficiency rates by subject and grade, subgroup (racial, income, disability) results and the size of achievement gaps, chronic absenteeism, ACT/SAT, and AP/College Credit Plus. See [Gaps / unverified](#gaps--unverified).

## How to read Ohio's report card

- **The rating system changed, so older and newer grades don't line up.** Before 2020, Ohio graded districts with A–F letters, and before that (through about 2012) with labels such as "Excellent," "Continuous Improvement" and "Academic Watch." No report card ratings were issued for 2019-20, and 2020-21 had none either, because of COVID-19 testing disruptions. Starting with the 2021-22 report card, Ohio rates five components on a 1–5 star scale. Starting with 2022-23, it also gives an **overall** star rating in half-star steps ([DEW, Report Card Guide 2024-25](https://education.ohio.gov/getattachment/Topics/Data/Report-Card-Resources/Traditional-Report-Cards/2024-2025-Report-Card-Guide.pdf.aspx?lang=en-US) [7]; [Axios Cleveland, 2023](https://www.axios.com/local/cleveland/2023/09/15/cleveland-schools-state-report-card) [8]). **A pre-2020 "D" and a post-2022 "2.5 stars" are not on the same scale.**
- **3 stars = "meets state standards."** 4 stars exceeds them, and 1–2 stars means the district needs support to meet them ([Axios, 2023](https://www.axios.com/local/cleveland/2023/09/15/cleveland-schools-state-report-card) [8]).
- **The six components:**
  - **Achievement:** how many students pass tests, summarized in the Performance Index.
  - **Progress:** value-added growth compared with what was expected for those same students.
  - **Gap Closing:** whether student subgroups meet targets.
  - **Graduation:** four- and five-year graduation rates, weighted together.
  - **Early Literacy:** K–3 reading.
  - **College, Career, Workforce & Military Readiness (CCWMR):** first rated in 2024-25 ([CH-UH 2025](https://www.chuh.org/p/~board/district-board-posts/post/ch-uh-announces-report-card-gains-for-2024-2025) [4]; [News 5, 2025](https://www.news5cleveland.com/news/local-news/ohio-school-report-cards-released-east-cleveland-and-akron-public-school-districts-see-improvements) [5]).
- **Achievement and Progress measure different things.** Achievement is closely tied to students' family income and other background factors. Progress tries to measure the school's effect on students' growth. A district can score low on Achievement and high on Progress at the same time, and from 2023-24 through 2025-26 CH-UH did.
- **Performance Index.** Each tested student earns points by performance level, from 0 for "limited" up to 1.2 for "advanced plus." So the maximum PI is 120 ([DEW Report Card Guide](https://education.ohio.gov/getattachment/Topics/Data/Report-Card-Resources/Traditional-Report-Cards/2024-2025-Report-Card-Guide.pdf.aspx?lang=en-US) [7]).

*Note on Progress (OpenHeights inference):* The district's 2025-26 release says Progress was 4 stars, "increased one star." That puts 2024-25 at 3 stars. That is the district's own description; we did not check it against state value-added data.

That suggests, but does not show, that CH-UH reached 3 stars or more that year. See Gaps.

## Performance Index trend

| Report card year | PI score | PI as % of 120 maximum *(OpenHeights calculation)* | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2023-24 | 69.2 | 69.2 ÷ 120 = 57.7% | [4] |
| 2024-25 | 74.6 | 74.6 ÷ 120 = 62.2% | [4] |
| 2025-26 | 79 | 79 ÷ 120 = 65.8% | [2] |

*OpenHeights calculation:* From 2023-24 to 2025-26, PI rose 79 − 69.2 = **9.8 points**, about 14% (9.8 ÷ 69.2 = 0.142). State test scores rose across Ohio over the same period. DEW reported that statewide ELA proficiency reached 62.2% in 2025-26, a five-year high ([Peak of Ohio, 2026](https://www.peakofohio.com/local-news/ohio-school-report-cards-show-gains-in-literacy-math-and-attendance/) [9]). Part of CH-UH's gain may therefore follow the statewide trend. Ohio's own "percent of maximum" for the Achievement component is computed against a different benchmark (the average of the top 2% of districts). The percentages above are a simple share of the 120-point maximum, not the state's official figure.

- The district says 92.4% was its highest weighted graduation rate under the star-rating format [4]. The separate four-year and five-year rates were not available in the sources we could reach. An undated state page snippet showed a 92.1% four-year rate, but we could not confirm which class year it covers (see Gaps).

- *OpenHeights calculation:* third-grade reading rose 68.9 − 64 = **4.9 percentage points** in one year. About 31% of third graders (100 − 68.9) were still below grade level in 2025-26.

## Longer trend (pre-2020 systems)

Before the star system, CH-UH's state ratings were low. Patch Cleveland Heights reported that the district received a **"D"** on the A–F report card ([Patch](https://patch.com/ohio/clevelandheights/ch-uh-schools-get-d-state-report-card) [11]; [Patch](https://patch.com/ohio/clevelandheights/chuh-schools-get-d-controversial-state-report-cards) [12]). Patch also reported that CH-UH ranked in the **bottom 5 percent** of districts on state tests ([Patch](https://patch.com/ohio/clevelandheights/ch-uh-ranked-in-bottom-5-percent-based-on-state-tests) [13]). We saw these only as article titles and could not open them, so the exact years and figures are not verified. Under the older labels used through about 2012, CH-UH is commonly described as having been rated "Continuous Improvement" during parts of the 2000s and 2010s. We did not confirm this for specific years, and it should not be cited until it is checked. The A–F letters, the old labels and today's stars measure different things on different scales and should not be read as one continuous series.

## Gaps / unverified

1. **Direct state data was not accessible.** The state report card site, chuh.org, cleveland.com, News 5 and Patch could not be fetched directly while this page was written. The figures above come from search-indexed excerpts of those pages, and every number should be checked on [reportcard.education.ohio.gov](https://reportcard.education.ohio.gov) [1] before publishing. The research also hit a search limit before these items could be finished.
2. **District IRN.** The assignment listed IRN 043687. The state's school IDs for CH-UH, such as OH-043794-015842 for Heights High in an [OHSAA listing](https://ohsaa.finalforms.com/state_districts/13684) [17], suggest the district IRN is **043794**. This needs checking.
3. **Undated state page.** A search excerpt of the state's district page for IRN 043794 showed: overall 3.5, Achievement 3, Progress 3, Gap Closing 4, **Early Literacy 2**, 61.8% (measure unclear), four-year graduation rate 92.1%. These do not match any single year in the district's releases (for example, the district reports Early Literacy at 3 stars in both 2024-25 and 2025-26). The excerpt may mix years. Not used in the tables above.
4. **2021-22 and 2022-23 ratings** for CH-UH were not found. The overall rating for 2022-23 is unknown, and the exact 2023-24 Progress rating is unknown.
5. **Not collected for this page:**
   - percent proficient by subject and grade, and state averages;
   - subgroup results (Black, white, multiracial, economically disadvantaged, students with disabilities) and the size of the racial achievement gap;
   - Progress ratings by subgroup;
   - chronic absenteeism;
   - ACT/SAT participation and scores;
   - AP and College Credit Plus participation;
   - industry credentials.

   The district's 5-star Gap Closing rating in 2025-26 measures whether subgroups met their targets. It does **not** show how large the gap between groups actually is.
6. **2025-26 elementary building ratings.** Search summaries of the district's release listed contradictory numbers. For example, Noble was shown in a "4-star" list and also as "2.5," and Oxford as 2.5. Not published here.
7. **Contradiction in the 2024-25 building count:** "8 of 10 schools met or exceeded" versus a list showing all 10 at 3 stars or more.
8. **Pre-2020 letter grades, "bottom 5 percent," and "Continuous Improvement"/"Academic Watch" designations**: years and details are not verified.
9. **District framing.** Both releases are titled "Report Card Gains." In 2025-26 the overall rating was flat at 3.5 and the Graduation, Early Literacy and CCWMR ratings were unchanged. The gains were in the Achievement, Progress and Gap Closing ratings and in the weighted graduation rate.
