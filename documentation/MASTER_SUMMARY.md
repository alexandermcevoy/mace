## 1. Demographics

### Population Trends

Cleveland Heights experienced gradual population decline over the study period:

- **2010 Census:** 46,238
- **2020 Census:** 45,312 (-2.0% from 2010)
- **2023 Estimate:** 44,694 (-1.4% from 2020)
- **Lowest point:** 2019 at 43,887

The 2020 Census showed a partial rebound, but population has continued declining since.

### Age Structure

The city maintains a relatively stable age distribution:
- **Under 15:** 16.3-16.5% (slightly below Ohio average)
- **Working Age (15-64):** 64.9-65.0% (stable)
- **65 and older:** 18.7-18.9% (above national average of 16%)
- **Median Age:** 36.4-36.5 years (below Ohio's 38.8)

**Policy Implication:** The aging population trend is modest but worth monitoring for service delivery planning.

### Racial/Ethnic Composition

Cleveland Heights is notably diverse:
- **White (non-Hispanic):** 47.9% (2023), down from 50.8% (2010)
- **Black (non-Hispanic):** 39.2% (stable)
- **Asian:** 5.5%, up from 4.5%
- **Two or more races:** Growing from 1.5% to 2.7%

**Policy Implication:** The city is becoming more diverse, with growing multiracial and Asian populations.

## 2. Income & Economics

### Household Income Trends

Median household income has grown substantially:
- **2015:** $52,853
- **2019:** $60,800 (pre-pandemic peak trajectory)
- **2023:** $72,302

This represents 36.8% nominal growth. Adjusted for inflation to 2023 dollars, the growth is more modest but still positive.

### Income Comparison

- Cleveland Heights 2023: $72,302
- Cuyahoga County: $62,823
- Ohio: $69,680
- USA: $74,580

Cleveland Heights is above county and state medians but slightly below national median.

### Poverty

- **Overall poverty rate:** Declined from 15.8% (2015) to 14.7% (2023)
- **Family poverty rate:** Declined from 10.2% to 8.5%
- **Comparison:** Ohio rate is 13.2%, so Cleveland Heights remains slightly higher

### Employment

- Unemployment peaked at 9.5% in 2020 (COVID impact)
- 2023 unemployment: 6.0%
- Recovery has been strong

## 3. Housing

### Housing Stock

- **Total units:** ~21,850-22,100 (relatively stable)
- **Vacancy rate:** 8.9% (2019) to 10.9% (2023) - trending up
- **Housing age:** 56.6% built before 1939 (oldest housing stock in region)

### Tenure

- **Owner-occupied:** 57-58% (slightly declining)
- **Renter-occupied:** 42-43%
- Geographic variation is significant (Coventry Village: high renter %; southeast neighborhoods: 90%+ ownership)

### Home Values

Strong appreciation, especially 2020-2022:
- **2015:** $128,500 median
- **2022:** $195,800 (peak)
- **2023:** $188,471 (-3.7% YoY correction)

The 2023 decline reflects regional and national market cooling.

## 4. Municipal Finance

### Revenue

General Fund revenue has grown consistently:
- **2015:** $48.5M
- **2023:** $66.3M (+36.7%)

**Income Tax Revenue** (largest source):
- **2015:** $32.5M (at 2.0% rate)
- **2016:** $35.2M (rate increased to 2.25%)
- **2023:** $45.8M

The 2015 voter-approved 0.25% income tax increase (effective 2016) significantly boosted revenue.

**Property Tax Revenue:**
- Growing modestly from $8.2M to $9.9M
- Benefiting from increased property valuations

### Key Revenue Sources (2023)
1. Income Tax: 69% of General Fund
2. Property Tax: 15%
3. Intergovernmental: 7%
4. Charges for Services: 5%
5. Other: 4%

### Expenditures

Major categories (2023):
1. **Police/Safety:** $18.5M (30%)
2. **Fire/EMS:** $12.8M (21%)
3. **Public Works:** $9.8M (16%)
4. **General Government:** $7.4M (12%)
5. **Parks & Recreation:** $4.0M (6%)
6. **Capital Outlay:** $3.9M (6%)
7. **Debt Service:** $3.2M (5%)
8. **Community Development:** $2.8M (4%)

### Budget Notes

- 2024 marked 5th consecutive year of General Fund revenue growth
- City received "Award with Distinction" from Ohio Auditor
- 2025 budget faced unusual political gridlock (first partial budget in city history)
- ARPA funds ($15M budgeted 2024) provided one-time federal support

## 5. Education

### Cleveland Heights-University Heights City School District

**Enrollment Trends:**
- **2015:** 5,450 students
- **2020:** 5,062 students
- **2023:** 5,010 students
- **Decline:** -8.1% over study period

### Demographics

The student body is significantly more diverse than the overall population:
- **Black:** 71.3% (vs. 39.2% citywide)
- **White:** 16.6% (vs. 47.9% citywide)
- **Economically Disadvantaged:** 62%

This disparity suggests differential school choice patterns by race/income.

### Staffing

- **Student-Teacher Ratio:** 14.3 (2023)
- **Teachers (FTE):** 351
- **Administrators:** 22

### Notable Events

- 2015-2017: Major high school renovation (students at Wiley campus)
- 2017-2018: Renovated building reopened

## Data Quality Notes

### High Confidence Data
- Population counts (Census decennial)
- Racial/ethnic composition (Census/ACS)
- Median household income (ACS)
- Housing tenure (ACS)
- Tax rates (official city documents)
- School enrollment totals (NCES/Ohio DOE)

### Moderate Confidence (Estimates/Interpolations)
- Year-by-year population between Census counts
- Some financial figures between ACFR years
- Home value trends (multiple source reconciliation)

### Data Gaps to Address
1. **Exact municipal revenue/expenditure figures** - Recommend downloading full ACFRs from Ohio Auditor
2. **Pre-2015 data** - Available but not yet compiled
3. **Granular school data** - Ohio DOE has detailed building-level data
4. **RITA collection data** - Annual reports may have municipality-specific figures
